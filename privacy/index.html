Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hard.Money (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and shares information when you use hard.money (the “Site”). By using the Site you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Information you provide: when you submit a funding request, contact form, lender application, supporter pledge, contest registration or job application, we collect the details you enter — such as name, email, phone, company, property and loan details, experience, estimated credit range and messages.
  • Automatically collected information: IP address, browser and device information, pages viewed, referring URLs and approximate location, collected via cookies and similar technologies by us and by third-party services described below.

How we use information

  • To route funding requests to lenders matched to your deal and to allow them to contact you.
  • To respond to inquiries, applications and pledges.
  • To operate, secure and improve the Site and measure content performance.
  • To display advertising, including interest-based advertising where permitted.
  • To comply with law and enforce our Terms.

How we share information

Funding requests are shared with one or more lenders or brokers whose lending criteria match your request so they can contact you about financing. Those lenders are independent businesses with their own privacy policies. Service providers — form processing, email delivery, hosting (GitHub Pages), analytics and advertising partners — process information on our behalf. We may disclose information to comply with law, protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transfer. We do not sell personal information to data brokers.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Embedded YouTube videos use youtube-nocookie.com and load only when you press play; YouTube may then set cookies under Google's privacy policy. We may also use privacy-respecting analytics to count visits. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling cookies may affect Site functionality.

Visitors from the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Where required, we request consent for personalized advertising through a consent management platform before serving personalized ads, and we serve non-personalized ads otherwise. Our legal bases for processing are consent, performance of a contract (responding to your request) and legitimate interests in operating the Site. You have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Your choices and rights

  • Access, correction, deletion: submit a “Privacy request” through our contact form. We respond within 30 days (45 for California residents where permitted).
  • Do Not Sell or Share: we do not sell personal information for money. Sharing a funding request with matched lenders is at your direction. To the extent interest-based advertising constitutes “sharing” under California law, you may opt out using the ad-settings links above or by submitting a privacy request.
  • Marketing contact: you may opt out of our emails via the unsubscribe link, and of texts by replying STOP. Lenders you have been matched with must be contacted directly to opt out of their communications.
  • California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, delete, correct and non-discrimination. We do not knowingly sell or share personal information of consumers under 16.

Data retention and security

We retain submissions for as long as needed for the purposes above and to meet legal obligations, then delete or anonymize them. Forms are transmitted over HTTPS. No method of transmission is perfectly secure.

Children

The Site is not directed to anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Third-party links

Links to lenders and other sites are governed by those sites' policies.

Changes

We will post changes on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions or requests: use the contact form and select “Privacy request”.
