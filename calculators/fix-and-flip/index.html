Reading the numbers

Cash deployed is your down payment plus points, interest, holding and closing costs — the money actually out of pocket. ROI divides profit by that figure; annualized return scales it to a year so a 7-month flip can be compared with other uses of capital. If the loan amount shows the ARV cap binding (loan < LTC × cost), the lender is signaling the purchase price is high relative to finished value. Compare against the 70%-rule max offer.
