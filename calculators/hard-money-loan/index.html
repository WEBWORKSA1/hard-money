How to read the results

The effective annualized cost converts points and fees into an equivalent annual rate for your actual hold period. On a 6-month loan, 2 points add roughly 4% to the effective rate; on a 12-month loan, 2%. This is why comparing two term sheets by rate alone is a mistake — see rates and points explained.

Questions to ask the lender

  • Is interest charged on the full loan or only on drawn funds?
  • Is there a minimum interest period (e.g., 3 months)?
  • What is the extension fee and how many extensions are allowed?
  • Which fees are netted from proceeds vs. paid at closing?
