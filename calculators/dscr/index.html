DSCR calculator for rental refinances

Planning to refinance a hard money loan into a 30-year DSCR rental loan? Check whether the rent covers the new payment. Lenders generally want 1.0–1.25; below 1.0 means lower leverage or a higher rate.

DSCR

—

Net operating income (annual)
Monthly P&I (30-yr)
Annual debt service (P&I)

Most DSCR lenders compute the ratio as rent ÷ PITIA with vacancy and management at 0%; this tool lets you stress-test with both. Leave them at 0 for the lender view.

Improving a thin DSCR

  • Reduce the loan amount (lower LTV) — the lever you control.
  • Buy down the rate with points.
  • Shop insurance; it is often the most inflated line in PITIA.
  • Document higher market rent with a rent survey before the appraisal.
  • Consider a 10-year interest-only DSCR option to lower the payment used in the ratio.
