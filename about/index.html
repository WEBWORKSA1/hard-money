About Hard.Money

An independent education and referral hub for hard money and private real estate lending — built because the information borrowers need was scattered across lender footnotes and forum threads.

What we do

Hard.Money publishes practitioner-level guides, calculators built on lender formulas, state-by-state law summaries and a lender directory that puts terms side by side. When you are ready, one funding request reaches multiple vetted lenders. We are free for borrowers and funded by lenders, sponsors and readers.

What we are not

We are not a lender, mortgage broker or financial adviser. We do not make credit decisions, hold funds or guarantee approval. Lenders you connect with set their own terms and are responsible for their own licensing and compliance.

Who is behind it

Hard.Money is operated by Webworks Media Network, an independent digital publisher that builds and runs focused, data-driven web properties. The editorial team draws on contributors with experience in private lending origination and underwriting, real estate investing, and real estate law; every published guide is reviewed by a second subject-matter expert under our editorial policy.

How we stay independent

  • Paid placements are always labeled.
  • Rankings and data are never sold; lenders cannot pay to change their listed terms.
  • We decline advertisers whose practices we would warn readers about.
  • Our revenue sources are disclosed in How we make money.

Corrections

Found an error? Tell us. We correct quickly, note material changes on the page, and credit the reader who flagged it if they wish.

Contact

Use the contact form for editorial, borrower or lender questions. For inquiries about this website, the domain name, sponsorship, advertising or partnership, use the link at the top of every page.
