Fix and Flip Loans

Short-term, interest-only financing that covers the purchase and 100% of the renovation budget for 1–4 unit properties you plan to resell.

🌉

Bridge Loans for Real Estate

Fast 6–24 month financing to acquire, reposition or cash out a property while you arrange permanent financing or a sale.

🏘️

DSCR Rental Loans

30-year investor loans qualified on the property’s rent-to-payment ratio instead of your personal income. The standard exit for BRRRR.

🏗️

Ground-Up Construction Loans

Financing for infill builders and spec developers: land plus vertical costs with a draw schedule tied to construction milestones.

🏢

Commercial Hard Money Loans

Asset-based loans on multifamily (5+ units), mixed-use, retail, office, industrial, hospitality and land when speed or story matters more than rate.

🛠️

Hard Money Loans With Bad Credit

What is realistic with a 550–640 score: which lenders look past credit, what they require instead, and how to improve your terms.

Land and Lot Loans From Private Lenders

Short-term financing for entitled lots, infill land and acreage with a clear development or resale plan.
