How to Finance a BRRRR Deal With Hard Money (and Refinance Out)

HMBy the Hard.Money editorial team·Published 2026-10-30·9 min readFact-checked

Advertiser disclosure: we may be compensated by lenders featured on this site; this does not influence our editorial content. Learn more.

Key takeaways

  • BRRRR is a two-loan structure: short-term hard money in, 30-year DSCR out.
  • The refinance is sized at 75–80% of new appraised value — the “cash-out” only works if all-in cost is below ~75% of ARV.
  • Line up the DSCR lender before the hard money closes and confirm its seasoning policy.
  • Rent must cover the new payment (DSCR ≥ 1.0–1.25) or the refinance shrinks.

BRRRR is a financing sequence, not a strategy

Buy, Rehab, Rent, Refinance, Repeat describes what happens to the property. What makes it work is the hand-off between two very different loans: a fix and flip (bridge) loan that tolerates distressed property and finances the renovation, and a DSCR rental loan that offers 30-year money on the finished, rented asset. Investors who plan both loans before buying recycle most of their capital; investors who plan only the first often end up stuck.

The math, with numbers

Purchase $160,000, rehab $40,000, ARV $280,000, market rent $2,300. Loan 1 (90% LTC, 75% ARV cap): $180,000, so $20,000 down plus about $5,000 points and costs and $6,000 of draw float. Six months of interest at 10.5% on an average $170,000 outstanding ≈ $8,900. All-in with carry ≈ $214,000. Loan 2 (DSCR at 75% LTV on $280,000): $210,000. Payoff $180,000 plus closing costs of about $7,000 leaves roughly $23,000 cash out — recovering most of the $31,000 you put in, with about $8,000 left in the deal against $70,000 of equity. The DSCR check: at 7.25% on $210,000 the P&I is about $1,433; add $450 taxes and insurance for PITIA ≈ $1,883. Rent ÷ PITIA = 1.22 — approvable at good pricing. Seasoning: the rule that breaks BRRRRs

Seasoning is how long you must own the property before a lender will lend against the new appraised value rather than your purchase price. Agency (Fannie/Freddie) cash-out refinances generally require 12 months (6 months in some cases). DSCR lenders vary: many allow cash-out on appraised value after 3 to 6 months, and a growing number require no seasoning if the renovation is documented with invoices and before/after photos. Ask the question explicitly before you choose a DSCR lender, and match your hard money term to the answer plus a 3-month cushion.

Pre-purchase checklist for a clean refinance

  • Obtain a DSCR pre-qualification or at least a program sheet showing LTV, min DSCR, seasoning and prepayment terms.
  • Verify market rent with a rent survey, not a guess — the appraiser's Form 1007 decides your DSCR.
  • Confirm the property type qualifies (DSCR lenders are picky about rural, condotel and 5+ units).
  • Keep the LLC and the title vesting consistent between loans to avoid transfer issues.
  • Document every rehab dollar: paid invoices, cancelled checks, permits, photos.
  • Protect your credit during the project — the DSCR lender will pull it again.

What to do if you get stuck

If the appraisal comes in low or rent misses, options in order: (1) rate-and-term refinance that pays off the hard money with less cash out; (2) a lower-leverage DSCR loan with a lower rate and a larger left-in balance; (3) extend the hard money loan 3–6 months and re-appraise after seasoning; (4) sell. Avoid the trap of extending twice at a point each — by then a sale usually pencils better.

Lenders that do both sides

Several lenders in our directory offer a fix-to-rent program: one approval, discounted points on the refinance, and no seasoning between the loans. The convenience is real; still compare the DSCR rate against standalone DSCR lenders, since captive takeouts are sometimes priced 25–50 bps wide.

Frequently asked questions

How much cash can I pull out of a BRRRR refinance?
Up to 75–80% of appraised value minus payoff and closing costs. If your all-in cost exceeds 75% of ARV, you will leave money in the deal.
Does the DSCR lender care about my income?
No. It cares about the property’s rent relative to the payment, your credit score and your reserves.
Can I BRRRR a property I already own free and clear?
Yes — a cash-out bridge loan funds the rehab, then a DSCR refinance takes it out.
